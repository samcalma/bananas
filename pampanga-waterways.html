Pampanga sits in an old lakebed

Before dikes and dams, most of the province was seasonal lake — the pinac. Rivers still trace the same low ground, and every rainy season the water tries to remember where it used to live. Toggle the layers to see the river system, the three historic swamp basins, and the towns built inside them.
