Arrive early

Timing

Leave extra time for Seaside Boulevard and EDSA traffic. If you're staying at the Conrad or the Wyndham properties, the walk to the Arena is short.

After the show

Crowds

Expect heavy crowds leaving the Arena and high demand for rides. Consider walking to a quieter spot along the bay or waiting out the first rush before you head for a ride.

Getting there

Transport

MOA sits on Seaside Boulevard in Pasay, on the Manila Bay side, at the southern tip of EDSA. Grab is the simplest option from most hotels; check traffic, since EDSA can be slow in rush hour and especially on concert nights.

Walking inside the complex

Layout

The Arena sits inside the Mall of Asia Complex, so the Conrad, SM by the Bay, and the Wyndham properties are all a short walk away. Hotels a little farther out, like Citadines and Hotel 101, are a longer walk or a short ride. Distances are on the Stay tab.

Rides after the show

Transport

Ride-hailing apps like Grab are the easiest option after the show, and it's worth setting pickup points in advance. Expect surge pricing and long waits right after the Arena empties. Walking a few blocks away from the crowd often gets you a faster pickup.

Install the app and add a payment method before you go. Pickup points and surge rules change, so check them on the day.

Drinking Water

Health

Stick to bottled or filtered water. Metro Manila's municipal supply is treated at the source, but quality after that depends on each building's pipes and storage tanks. Bottled water is cheap and sold everywhere, and most hotels provide complimentary bottles.

Heat and sun

Weather

February is the dry season in Manila, so expect hot, humid afternoons and little rain. Bring water and sun protection for the walk between hotels, the mall, and the Arena. The Arena is indoors, so the heat matters most on the way there and back.

General seasonal guidance, not a forecast for Feb 7, 2027.

Payments

Money

Cards are accepted at the hotels, malls, and most restaurants. Carry some cash for smaller cafes, food stalls, and tips. GCash is widely used for payments in the Philippines.

Apple Pay launched in the Philippines on 4 August 2026. At launch, cards from Chinabank, GoTyme Bank, Metrobank, and UnionBank work with it. Ayala Malls and Jollibee are among the merchants listed at launch.

Apple's launch list does not name SM Malls or the Arena. Check the terminal or contactless logo before relying on Apple Pay at a specific shop. Sources only confirm Philippine-issued Visa, Mastercard, and GoTyme cards. Visitors should check with their own card issuer whether a foreign card works.
