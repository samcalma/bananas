Getting there

Transport

MOA sits on Seaside Boulevard in Pasay, on the Manila Bay side, at the southern tip of EDSA. Grab is the simplest option from most hotels; check traffic, since EDSA can be slow in rush hour.

Crowds

Timing

Sources say the mall is packed on weekends and holidays. Weekday visits are easier.

Check ahead
