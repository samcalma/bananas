Hikes

Hong Kong hides real trails between the towers. Dragon's Back is the one to do.

Do this one

Dragon's Back 龍脊

Shek O Country Park · Hong Kong Trail, Section 8
8.5km
2–5 hrs
Beginner–moderate MTR accessible Ends at a beach Ridge / ocean views No shops on trail
  • Undulating ridgeline that resembles a dragon's spine — panoramic views of Shek O, Big Wave Bay, and the South China Sea
  • Only a ~30 min uphill walk from the MTR to the trailhead — no car needed from TST
  • Finishes at Big Wave Bay beach, where there are cafés and food — good payoff to end on
  • Well-signposted throughout; sturdy trainers or boots recommended for the steeper parts

Before you go

Practical notes for a group morning out
  • Timing: start before 9 AM or avoid weekends — it's popular and gets crowded on the ridge
  • Bring: water and snacks — there's nothing on the trail itself until Big Wave Bay
  • Weather: mid-Jan is cool and dry (14–19°C) — ideal hiking conditions, pack a light layer for the exposed ridge
  • Getting there: MTR to Shau Kei Wan, then bus/taxi to To Tei Wan trailhead

Fits naturally as a swap for — or add-on to — the Peak/Central or Lantau day, depending on the group's appetite for a real walk.

Other trails if the group wants variety: Victoria Peak / HK Trail Section 1 (flat, easy, pairs with the Peak Tram day), Lantau Peak or Sunset Peak (tougher climbs, sunrise/sunset views), MacLehose Section 2 (13.5 km coastal, bigger day, further from TST).

Hong Kong Disneyland

Lantau Island · reachable by MTR Disneyland Resort line
~$185
1-day / adult
World of Frozen Mystic Manor Full-day (10 AM–8 PM) Smaller than Tokyo/US parks Frozen rides: 60–90 min waits
  • World of Frozen — the world's first and largest Frozen-themed land; Frozen Ever After is the standout ride
  • Other must-dos: Mystic Manor, Big Grizzly Mountain Runaway Mine Cars, Festival of the Lion King, and the Momentous nighttime show
  • Smaller than other Disney parks but doable in one full day; a 2-day pass only makes sense if staying at a Disney hotel
  • Consider Premier Access (per-ride skip) or the 3-ride pass to beat the Frozen queues — buy early in the app, prices climb through the day

Ticket ~US$184–187/adult via resellers; Trip.com lists standalone days from ~$76 on promo. Not live-rechecked for Jan 2027 — confirm at booking. For 7 people, factor the group total plus any skip-the-line passes.
